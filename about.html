About TeleSync

TeleSync began with a simple observation: in a video consultation, a doctor can see and hear the patient, but cannot examine them. We are building the missing bridge, so that distance no longer decides the quality of a check-up.

Our Mission

Healthcare professionals in telemedicine often rely mainly on video calls and patient-reported symptoms, without enough objective examination data. Our mission is to bridge that care gap by extending real examination capability to rural, underserved, elderly and homebound populations, using a portable device and the smartphone people already own.

Our Story

Three of us, three paths to the same idea. In our own words, here is what inspired each of us to create TeleSync.

Kundol “Canoe” Tangruthaiwanich

Ruamrudee International School, Grade 12

Founder and Lead Engineer

I grew up with vernal keratoconjunctivitis, a chronic allergic eye disease that causes itching, redness and painful sensitivity to light. Keeping it under control meant years of regular visits to an eye specialist, often just so a doctor could look at my eyes and decide whether my treatment needed to change. I was lucky that a specialist was always within reach. Many people living with long-term conditions are not, and for them every follow-up can mean a long, costly journey, or no follow-up at all. That is what drives my work on TeleSync: if the examination can travel to the patient, ongoing care no longer has to depend on how close someone lives to a hospital.

Burich “BenJ” Benjarattananon

Bangkok Christian College, Grade 12

Research and Development Specialist

My experiences in scientific research and representing Thailand in one of the world’s most prestigious science and engineering competitions, as well as many international innovation exhibitions, shaped my approach to solving real-world problems. Years of research taught me how to apply scientific concepts to practical solutions that could make a real difference in society. My interests began to shift toward medicine and through my volunteer work in hospitals and underprivileged communities I saw first hand the challenges of healthcare accessibility. I am thrilled to be part of the TeleSync team, bringing together my research background and these experiences to work alongside passionate individuals in making healthcare more accessible.

Pakawat “Messi” Poldetch

Ruamrudee International School, Grade 11

Chief Commercial Officer

My antibiotics research in aquaculture helped me see how biological research connects to the health and livelihoods of communities. Developing an LLM-integrated RAG ChatBot then showed me the value of translating scientific knowledge into practical tools that people can use. As my interests in biology became increasingly focused on medicine and human health, I wanted to bring that same approach to healthcare. This inspired me to aid in the development of Telesync, providing a way to connect patients and healthcare professionals through accessible technology; thus, allowing me to extend my motivation to support communities from aquaculture to human care.
